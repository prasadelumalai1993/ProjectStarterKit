# Design Choices

Key decisions and the reasoning behind them. **Append new entries; don't rewrite old ones when a decision changes** — add a new entry that supersedes the old one and says why. The history of *why we changed our minds* is often more useful later than the current state alone.

Each entry should answer: what did we decide, what did we consider instead, and why did we pick this. Skip decisions that are obvious or easily reversible — this file is for the ones worth remembering the reasoning behind.
